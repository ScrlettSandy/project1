Curated Frames

Curated Frames is a film-centered website concept made to feel cinematic, polished, and personal. It brings together movie browsing, visual storytelling, and editorial-inspired design.

Visit Website View Gallery
Project Overview

A cinematic space for exploring film.

This project was designed around my love of film and visual storytelling. The goal was to create a website that feels curated and immersive, while still being simple to move through. I focused on strong visuals, clear hierarchy, and a mood that reflects the feeling of watching and collecting films.

Open the live Curated Frames website

What I Did

I developed the concept, branding, layout, and overall visual direction for the website. I wanted each page to feel connected through consistent spacing, typography, colour, and image use.

Design Goal

My main goal was to make the site feel like more than a movie catalog. I wanted it to feel personal, atmospheric, and thoughtfully organized.

My Process

From concept to interface.

1

Concept

I started with the idea of making a personal film space that felt curated instead of plain.

2

Branding

I used a cinematic mood, strong imagery, and editorial-style typography to shape the identity.

3

Layout

I built the pages around clear sections so the site would stay easy to browse.

4

Refinement

I adjusted spacing, image placement, and hierarchy to make the site feel more polished.

Reflection

What I learned.

Curated Frames helped me combine film, branding, and web design into one project. It made me think about how a website can feel emotional and visually expressive while still being clear and user-friendly.
