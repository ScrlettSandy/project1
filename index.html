Curated Frames

Curated Frames is a film-centered website concept designed as a personal space to collect, organize, and explore movies through a visually curated experience. I wanted the site to feel cinematic, elevated, and easy to navigate while still reflecting my own love of film and visual storytelling.

Project Overview

A film website built to feel curated, cinematic, and personal.

Curated Frames is a website I designed around my love of film and visual storytelling. The idea was to create a digital space where users could search, sort, and reflect on movies in a way that feels thoughtful and immersive. I wanted the experience to feel editorial and polished while still being clear and easy to explore.

What I Did

I developed the concept, shaped the branding, designed the interface, and built out the layout structure for the site. I focused on how content could be organized visually while still maintaining a strong and recognizable identity.

Focus Areas

  • User experience and navigation
  • Brand identity and visual tone
  • Editorial-inspired layout design
  • Film-centered content organization
  • Creating an immersive browsing experience
My Process

Designing the experience from concept to interface.

I approached this project by thinking about both mood and usability. Since the concept was rooted in film, I wanted the website to feel visually rich and cinematic, but I also needed it to stay organized and user-friendly.

1

Concept

I started with the idea of creating a space where films could be explored in a curated and visually engaging way rather than through a plain catalog-style layout.

2

Branding

I built the identity around a cinematic and editorial tone, using typography, poster imagery, and structured layouts to give the site a distinct personality.

3

Interface Design

I focused on creating a homepage and supporting pages that felt polished, organized, and easy to move through while still keeping the experience visually strong.

4

Refinement

I refined the layout, spacing, and hierarchy so the site would feel cohesive and intentional, balancing personality with usability.

Design Choices

What shapes the site’s visual identity.

Cinematic Tone

The design is meant to feel film-centered from the start, using imagery and layout choices that create a more immersive and mood-driven experience.

Editorial Layout

I wanted the site to feel elevated and curated, so I used structured sections and clean visual hierarchy to make the interface feel intentional.

Poster Imagery

Film posters and visual previews help anchor the concept and make the browsing experience feel richer and more visually engaging.

User Experience

Even though the site is highly visual, usability stayed important. I focused on making the layout easy to read and navigate.

Personal Branding

The project reflects my own taste and interest in combining strong concepts with polished digital design.

Content Organization

A major part of the design was thinking about how film-related content could be grouped, sorted, and explored in a way that feels curated rather than overwhelming.

Reflection

What I learned from this project.

Curated Frames gave me the chance to combine my interest in film with my interest in digital design. It helped me think more deeply about how an interface can feel emotional, organized, and strongly branded at the same time.


More than anything, this project reflects the kind of work I want to keep making: thoughtful digital experiences built around strong concepts, visual identity, and user-centered design. It brought together branding, UX thinking, and visual storytelling in a way that felt both personal and polished.
